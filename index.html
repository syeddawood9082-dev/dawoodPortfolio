About Me

Streaming data and
lakehouse platforms

I am Dawood Syed, a Senior Data Engineer based in High Point, North Carolina. I build streaming data pipelines and lakehouse platforms that keep data fresh and ready for SQL access. At Cotiviti I work on Data Mountain Cloud, running Spark Structured Streaming into Apache Hudi tables on S3 with Trino queries and Kafka and Debezium change data capture under Airflow orchestration. Before Cotiviti I spent over six years at Prysmian building SAP and Snowflake data platforms. My core stack is Apache Spark, Apache Hudi, Trino, Apache Kafka, Debezium, Airflow, Snowflake, Java, Spring Boot, Python, and SQL.
