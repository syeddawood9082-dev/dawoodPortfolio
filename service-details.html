Service Overview

AI and technology consulting goes beyond writing code -- it involves understanding complex business requirements, designing scalable architectures, and ensuring systems are reliable, secure, and production-ready. I offer comprehensive consulting services that help organizations leverage AI/ML, cloud infrastructure, and data engineering to solve real-world challenges. From feasibility analysis and system design to implementation and optimization, I work closely with teams to deliver measurable impact.

1. AI/ML System Design

Designing and deploying reliable AI systems that bridge theoretical research and production environments. This includes model selection, training pipeline design, evaluation frameworks, and deployment strategies for scalable ML infrastructure.

2. Cloud & Distributed Architecture

Building secure, scalable cloud architectures using AWS, Azure, and hybrid environments. Expertise in distributed computing patterns, microservices design, and infrastructure-as-code for enterprise-grade deployments.

3. Data Pipeline Engineering

Designing scalable data pipelines using Apache Kafka, ETL frameworks, and real-time analytics platforms. Optimizing data flow for high-throughput processing and predictive analytics at scale.
